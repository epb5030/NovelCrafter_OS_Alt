# 🚀 OpenCrafter Studio: First-Time User & Author Walkthrough

Welcome to **OpenCrafter Studio**! This step-by-step guide walks you through setting up your first novel project, configuring your AI co-writer provider, building your world codex, mapping location cartography, tracking plot beats, and compiling a manuscript for publication.

---

## 📍 Step 1: Launch OpenCrafter

1. Start OpenCrafter locally:

```bash
node backend/dist/index.js
```

1. Open your web browser to **`http://localhost:3005`**.

---

## ✍️ Step 2: Create Your First Project

1. Click **"+ Create New Novel"** on the Project Dashboard.
1. Fill out:
   - **Novel Title**: e.g., *The Cartographer of Eldoria*
   - **Genre**: High Fantasy / Sci-Fi / Mystery
   - **Premise / Summary**: Briefly describe the central dramatic conflict.
1. Click **"Launch Writing Studio"**.

---

## 🤖 Step 3: Configure Your AI Co-Writer Engine

1. Go to **Settings** in the left navigation sidebar.
1. Pick your preferred AI Provider:
   - **Ollama (Local Offline)**: 100% private writing on host machine (`http://localhost:11434`).
   - **Google Gemini**: Paste your Google AI Studio key for massive 1M+ token context memory.
   - **OpenAI / Anthropic / OpenRouter**: Paste your provider key.
1. Set your author style guidelines:
   - **Point of View**: Third Person Limited / First Person
   - **Tense**: Past Tense
   - **Tone**: Dramatic, Immersive, Atmospheric

---

## 📖 Step 4: Build Your World Codex

1. Navigate to **Codex** tab.
1. Add key story entries:
   - **Characters**: Name, role, voice traits, catchphrases, speech register.
   - **Locations**: Fortress, city, or ruin details.
   - **Lore / Items**: Magic system rules, artifacts, factions.
1. In the Write Editor, type `@` to mention any Codex entry inline for instant lore reference cards.

---

## 🗺️ Step 5: Map World Cartography & Character Journeys

1. Navigate to **World Map** tab.
1. Click **"Auto-Map Codex Locations"** to convert your Codex locations into pins on the parchment canvas.
1. Drag location pins to adjust their geographical positions.
1. Select a character and click location pins in order to trace their color-coded journey path across chapters.
1. Use the **Spatial Travel Calculator** to compute travel distances (miles/leagues) and estimated travel duration in days based on travel mode (*foot, horseback, carriage, ship, wyvern flight, portal*).

---

## 🎤 Step 6: Tune Character Voice Distinctiveness

1. Open a scene in **Write** tab.
1. Click **"Voice Doctor"** in the toolbar.
1. Inspect spoken quote attribution, dialogue ratios, and character distinctiveness health scores.
1. Use the **AI Dialogue Tuner** to rewrite lines so they match each character's unique vocabulary and speech cadence.

---

## 📦 Step 7: Compile E-Book & Word Submission Document

1. Click **"Export Project"** in the top navigation bar.
1. Choose your publishing format:
   - **EPUB 3 E-Book**: Pick a typography theme (*Classic Garamond, Modern Sans, Vintage Typewriter*).
   - **Microsoft Word (.docx)**: Select Standard Shunn Literary Submission format or Clean Reading Draft.
1. Click **"Compile & Download"**.
